# Surface: recipes

Working documents, not food essays. A short opening that says why this cut and this method, then ingredients and steps a tired cook can follow. Metric units, Australian terms. Provenance and nutrition claims follow the claim policy like everywhere else — a recipe is where "nutrient-dense" sneaks in unsourced.

One internal link minimum, usually to the produce or the paddock story behind it.
